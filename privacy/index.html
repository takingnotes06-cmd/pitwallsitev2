Support Privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

privacy.

pitwall. is a race-engineering app for club and amateur racers. This explains what happens to your information when you use it.

the short version.

  • There's no pitwall. account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your garage, setups, events, laps, recordings and videos are stored on your iPhone. With pitwall Pro and iCloud sync turned on, your data (not your videos) is also stored in your own private iCloud, which only you can access. We can't see any of it.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't collect it.

what the app uses, and why.

Location

Used while the lap timer is running, to time laps and record your line around the track, and, if you choose "Use my location", to find the weather where you are. If you've allowed location, the app also compares where you are with its built-in list of tracks to tell you when an event is live at the track you're at. Lap timing can carry on in the background during a run. Location stays on your device, except as below for the weather.

Bluetooth and local network

Used only to connect to the GPS receivers (such as RaceBox) and OBD-II readers you choose to add. The data they send (position, motion, engine data, fault codes, and recordings stored on a RaceBox) is kept with your sessions on your device.

Files and Photos

When you import a data log (AiM, VBOX or CSV) or add an on-board video, pitwall. opens only the item you choose and keeps a copy on your iPhone. Videos from Photos come through the system picker, so pitwall. never has access to your photo library. When it reads data shown on a video (lap times, speed, g), that reading happens on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Saving to Photos

Add-only access, used only when you tap "Save to Photos" on an exported report. The app can't read your photo library.

Notifications

All of pitwall.'s notifications are created on your iPhone: reminders, results, weather, maintenance and occasional tips about features you haven't tried. Nothing is sent to us. Turn them off in your iPhone's Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ pitwall.

Weather

To show the forecast for a track, the app sends that track's coordinates (or your current location if you chose it) to Apple Weather. No information that identifies you is sent. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that request.

MYLAPS Speedhive results

When you import or link an event, the app reads public event results from Speedhive's results service: event lists by date and country, and the sessions, classifications and lap times for the events you choose. Your entry is found on your iPhone by matching your transponder, race number, class or name against the published results. None of your pitwall. data is sent to MYLAPS.

Purchases

pitwall Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app remembers on your iPhone whether you have Pro, so iCloud sync can start straight away after reinstalling.

Track data

Built-in start/finish positions come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). No information goes to OpenStreetMap from the app.

children.

pitwall. isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

deleting your data.

Delete the app to remove everything stored on your iPhone. If you use iCloud sync, you can remove pitwall.'s iCloud data in Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Account Storage.

changes.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
