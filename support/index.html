pro & purchases.

How do I restore pitwall Pro on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen or in Settings.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions ▸ pitwall Pro ▸ Cancel Subscription. Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

icloud sync.

My data isn't syncing between devices
  • iCloud sync is part of pitwall Pro, on every device.
  • Use the same Apple Account on each device.
  • Check you have iCloud storage free: Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud.
  • pitwall. Settings ▸ iCloud sync shows the status and any problem.
Are my on-board videos synced?

No. Videos stay on the iPhone you added them on, to save your iCloud space. Your laps, data and settings sync.

lap timer.

The lap timer isn't timing laps
  • Pick the track you're at; laps are timed when you cross its start/finish line.
  • Allow location for pitwall., with Precise Location on.
  • Mount the phone with a clear view of the sky, or use a RaceBox for 25 Hz GPS.
Which RaceBox devices work?

RaceBox Mini, Mini S and Micro, with pitwall Pro. Add them in Tools ▸ Manage devices.

My track isn't listed

With Pro you can add your own track and its start/finish line. Or email us the track's name and we'll add it.

data & video.

What data files can I import?

AiM Race Studio CSV exports, VBOX .vbo files and GPS CSV logs. For AiM .xrk or .xrz files, export a CSV from Race Studio first.

How do I line up my on-board video?

Add the video to a session in Data analysis. pitwall. lines it up by the time it was recorded, or by a lap timer shown on the video. You can fine-tune it under Video options ▸ Sync.

Results didn't import from Speedhive

Results appear once the circuit uploads them. Check your transponder, race number or name on the vehicle matches what's on the timing sheet.
